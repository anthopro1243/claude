# Collect the SAT Project into one folder

Each script searches your computer for anything SAT-related and copies it into
`Desktop/SAT_Project`, sorted into numbered folders with an index file.
The originals stay where they are.

## Mac
Open Terminal and run:

    bash ~/Downloads/collect_sat_project_mac.sh

## Windows
Open PowerShell and run:

    powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "$HOME\Downloads\collect_sat_project_windows.ps1"

## Then
1. Open `Desktop/SAT_Project/CHAT_TRANSCRIPT.md` and paste the whole old chat into it.
2. Look through `00_REVIEW_recent_downloads` and delete anything unrelated.
3. In the new account, upload the folder's files to a chat or Project and say:
   "Read CLAUDE_START_HERE.md and continue the SAT Project."
